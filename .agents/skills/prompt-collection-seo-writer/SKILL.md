# Prompt Collection SEO Writer Skill (ImagineBuddy Edition)

This skill instructs the agent to analyze target keywords, product photography niches, competitor hubs, and e-commerce commercial visual workflows to generate complete, high-ranking, and high-converting data payloads for ImagineBuddy's **Prompt Collections Management** (`/panel/admin/prompts/create` and `/panel/admin/prompts/edit/{id}`).

The resulting public page is served at **`https://imaginebuddy.com/prompts/{slug}`**, acting as an authoritative, indexed thematic hub designed to dominate traditional search engine result pages (Google, Bing) and AI chatbot retrieval systems (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity AI, Google Gemini SGE, Claude, Microsoft Copilot).

## 1. Core Platform Architecture & Business Philosophy

Every field, headline, bullet point, metadata description, and FAQ generated by this skill must embody ImagineBuddy's exact business model and value proposition:

### The Core ImagineBuddy Identity:
> **Imaginebuddy is an AI-powered commercial product photography prompt platform that helps e-commerce brands, Amazon sellers, D2C businesses, and creative agencies transform ordinary product photos into professional, studio-quality visual concepts using structured, adaptive AI prompts in the ChatGPT and Google Gemini.**

### The Dual Friction ImagineBuddy Eliminates:
1. **The Physical Photoshoot Bottleneck**: Studio rentals, photographer retainers ($2,000–$10,000+), lighting crews, prop sourcing, physical set styling, and weeks of scheduling delay.
2. **The Generic AI Prompt Guessing Problem**: Raw, unstructured prompts in AI generators hallucinate labels, warp packaging geometry, distort brand logos, shift SKU colors, and waste hundreds of generation credits.

### The ImagineBuddy Solution: Structured, Adaptive Prompts in ChatGPT & Google Gemini:
- **How It Works**:
  1. The user uploads an **ordinary product photo** (taken on a smartphone, plain backdrop, or white table).
  2. The user copies ImagineBuddy's **structured, adaptive prompt formula** tailored for this exact product category.
  3. The prompt is deployed directly inside **ChatGPT or Google Gemini** alongside the uploaded product image.
  4. The AI generator preserves the real physical product (silhouette, proportions, cap finishes, labels, brand colors) while generating **studio-quality, professional commercial visual concepts** (macro close-ups, editorial staging, bathroom vanities, outdoor lifestyles, and Amazon-compliant white packshots).
- **The Core Brand Promise**:
  > **"Your Product. Smarter Prompts. Better Product Photography."**  
  > *Turn an ordinary product photo into professional, studio-quality visual concepts using structured, adaptive AI prompts in ChatGPT and Google Gemini.*

## 2. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & Search Engine Strategy

To ensure collection hubs rank #1 on Google and get cited as authoritative sources by AI search engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google Gemini SGE, Copilot):

### A. Entity-First Technical Photography & AI Engine Vocabulary
Chatbots and search engines favor deep domain entity terms. Every collection payload must incorporate precise commercial studio and multimodal AI terminology:
- **Multimodal AI Integration**: `ChatGPT image input`, `Google Gemini multimodal vision staging`, `structured prompt syntax`, `adaptive subject placement`, `product anchor constraints`.
- **Studio Lighting Rigs**: `octabox diffused strobe`, `high-contrast specular rim light`, `soft ambient caustics`, `5600K daylight balance`, `diffused bounce fill`, `edge rim highlights`, `natural ground shadow falloff`.
- **Optics & Focal Lengths**: `85mm commercial portrait prime`, `f/2.8 commercial depth of field`, `100mm macro telephoto`, `subtle cinematic bokeh`, `50mm neutral distortion-free perspective`.
- **Surfaces, Sets & Pedestals**: `matte acrylic podium`, `polished Carrara marble slab`, `brushed anodized aluminum riser`, `travertine stone cube`, `textured velvet backdrop`, `water ripple reflection pool`.
- **Marketplace Technical Specs**: `Amazon pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255)`, `85%+ product frame fill ratio`, `zero color cast`, `high-resolution 1:1 square aspect ratio`.

### B. Information Gain & Direct Answerability for LLMs
- AI search algorithms extract concise, definitive explanations. Avoid vague fluff. Provide concrete technical mechanics: *how* adaptive prompts in ChatGPT and Google Gemini anchor packaging geometry, *why* structured lighting parameters prevent label smudging, and *how* negative space is engineered for Amazon infographic callouts.

### C. Natural Structural Hierarchy
- **H1 (`title`)**: Direct match to primary commercial search intent (e.g., `Jewelry & Necklace Photography Prompts`, `Saree Commercial Photography AI Prompts`).
- **Summary**: Immediate 2-sentence entity definition that search engines pull as rich meta snippets or LLM summary answers.
- **FAQs**: Formatted to answer the exact long-tail queries users type into Google and chatbots (commercial rights, ChatGPT and Gemini compatibility, packaging warping prevention).
